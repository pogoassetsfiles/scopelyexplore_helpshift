**Issue Description**
Some Trainers may receive a “You’re too far away” error when attempting to check in to a Meetup from Pokémon GO, even when they are at the Meetup location. This may prevent them from completing check-in.

 **Issue Status**
Investigating

 **Workaround**

* Enable precise location for the browser you use to access Campfire.
 **iOS:** Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > select the browser used for check-in > turn on Precise Location.
 **Android:** Ensure location permission is enabled for the browser used for check-in. If your device shows a Use precise location option, turn it on.
* Try checking in through [Campfire on the web](https://campfire.nianticlabs.com/discover).
* If you are still unable to check in, try using the [Campfire app](https://campfire.nianticlabs.com/en/).
* If the issue continues, contact us through in-game Support for further assistance.

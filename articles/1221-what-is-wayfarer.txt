[**Scopely Explore Wayfarer**](https://wayfarer.nianticlabs.com/)is a one-of-a-kind community mapping program that allows you to directly shape your real-world adventures across all Scopely Explore games. By participating in Scopely Explore Wayfarer, Explorers become part of an interactive community and contribute to the development of the Scopely Explore real-world map, which is the foundation for all Scopely Explore games.

As a Wayfarer Explorer, you can nominate new Wayspots in your community, review others’ nominations, and contribute information or edits to existing Wayspots to help the Scopely Explore map evolve in tandem with the real-world.

Earn Wayfarer rewards and get involved with a lively global mapping community that loves discovering new Wayspots around the world that inspire exercise, exploration, and social interaction.

Pokémon GO players Level 35+ are eligible to participate in the program. More Scopely Explore games may be eligible to contribute to the Wayfarer program in the future.

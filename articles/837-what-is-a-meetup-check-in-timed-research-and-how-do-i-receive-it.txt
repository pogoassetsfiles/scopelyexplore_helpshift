**Meetup Check-in Timed Research**  is exclusive Timed Research available at eligible events hosted by Community Ambassadors and other select partners. Eligible events display a  **Rewards**  icon on the Meetup invite.

RSVP to an eligible Meetup, visit the Meetup location, and check in to receive the associated Meetup Check-in Timed Research in Pokémon GO.

## **How to find a Meetup**

* Pokémon GO Map: From the Nearby menu, tap  **Map**  to view Meetup locations in your area.
* Event Calendar: Open the in-game Event Calendar and tap  **Join a Meetup** .
* Campfire: You can also browse nearby events through Campfire.

## **How to check in**

**Check-in opens 15 minutes before the Meetup begins and remains available until the Meetup ends.** You must be signed in and be at the Meetup location when checking in.

There are two primary ways to check in to a Community Ambassador Meetup.

### **Method 1: Check in directly from Pokémon GO**

You can check in directly from the Pokémon GO Map.

1. Open the Nearby menu and tap  **Map** .

2. Find the Meetup's  **purple calendar pin** .

3. Tap the pin and confirm that the Meetup is marked with  **Hosted by a Community Ambassador** .

4. Once check-in opens, tap  **Check In** .

If you arrive more than 15 minutes before the Meetup begins, you will see  **View Meetup**  instead. Once check-in opens, reopen the Meetup card and the  **Check In**  button will appear.

![image](../data/images/51446a21-8207-4411-80ca-1f26587e8064.png)

### **Method 2: Check in from your browser**

You can also check in through the Pokémon GO Map in your browser.

1. Go to https://pokemongo.com/en/map.

2. Make sure  **Events & Meetups**  is enabled.

3. Select the Meetup's  **purple calendar pin** .

4. Select  **Check In** .

![image](../data/images/cc8a3752-231d-4060-8080-25eec50ef70c.png)

If you receive a  **“You’re too far away”**  message even though you are at the Meetup location, try the following:

* Make sure  **Precise Location**  is enabled for the browser you are using.

* **iOS:**  Go to  **Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services** , select your browser, and turn on  **Precise Location** .
* **Android:**  Make sure location permission is enabled for your browser. If available, turn on  **Use precise location** .
* Try checking in again throughhttps://campfire.nianticlabs.com/discover[Campfire on the web](https://campfire.nianticlabs.com/discover).
* If you are still unable to check in, try using thehttps://campfire.nianticlabs.com/en/[Campfire app](https://campfire.nianticlabs.com/en/).
* If the issue continues, contact us through in-game Support.

## **After checking in**

After you successfully check in, open Pokémon GO. You should receive an in-app notification confirming that you are eligible to receive Meetup Check-in Timed Research. Check the  **Timed Research**  section to confirm that the research has been added.

You must claim the initial  **500 XP within one hour of checking in**  to receive the full Meetup Timed Research.

The Timed Research and bonuses available may vary depending on the event. Check the  **Happening Now**  section of the Event Calendar for the latest information.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **How many times can I receive Meetup Check-in Timed Research?**

You can receive Meetup Check-in Timed Research once per day.

### **I received a different Timed Research than I expected. What should I do?**

If you believe you received a different Timed Research than expected, check with the Community Ambassador hosting the Meetup. The Community Ambassador can confirm whether the correct event reward was configured.

### **Why isn’t my Timed Research showing up?**

Please make sure that:

* You successfully selected Check In for the Meetup.
* The Meetup is eligible for check-in rewards and displays a Rewards icon.
* You received a notification confirming that your check-in was successful.
* You claimed the initial 500 XP.
* You restarted Pokémon GO.

If the steps above do not resolve the issue, please submit an in-game support ticket for further assistance.

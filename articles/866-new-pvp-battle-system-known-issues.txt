Here is a list of issues related to the new PvP battle system for GO Battle League that are currently being investigated by our engineering team. This list will be updated regularly, and issues will be added and removed as they are discovered and resolved.

Please note that this is not a comprehensive list of all issues we are aware of or working to fix. You can find the list of known issues unrelated to GO Battle League [here](https://scopelyexplore.helpshift.com/hc/section/116-release-notes-known-issues/).

To report a bug that isn’t listed here, or get one on one support, please submit a ticket in-app under the Settings menu, or see [our article](https://scopelyexplore.helpshift.com/hc/faq/821-how-to-contact-support/) on how to best contact us for different issues.

While we work on fixing these issues, please try these tips to mitigate some of these issues:

* Avoid enabling  **“Auto-Join”**  for different Wi-Fi networks, as your device may switch networks during a battle and interrupt your connection.
* Avoid playing in areas where the internet connection is unstable, such as on subways or underground.

**Last updated** : September 11, 2026

## Open Issues

Adverse network conditions may cause unexpected gameplay issues

**Issue Description:**  When battling in adverse network conditions, game actions may behave in unexpected ways. Some examples include, but are not limited to, the battle becoming unresponsive immediately after switching Pokémon at the start of battle, and Pokémon switched in not displaying correctly.

**Issue Status:** Investigating

Trying to activate a Charged Attack immediately after switching a Pokémon in may instead activate a Fast Attack

**Issue Description:**  When a Trainer switches in a Pokémon that has previously charged up its Charged Attack and immediately tries to activate the Charged Attack, a Fast Attack may activate instead, delaying the activation of the Charged Attack

**Issue Status:**  Investigating

## Fixed in future release

Battle may be recorded as a loss when matchmaking fails with "Opponent has quit the battle"

**Issue Description:**  Trainers may be incorrectly granted a loss if the matchmaking process fails with "Opponent has quit the battle"

**Issue Status:**  Fixed in future release

The active Pokémon may be switched out after using a Charged Attack without Trainer input

**Issue Description:**  Immediately after a Trainer’s active Pokémon completes a Charged Attack, that Pokémon may be unexpectedly switched out with another Pokémon in the Trainer’s party.

**Issue Status:**  Fixed in future release

Some Trainers may find that their screen freezes right before a Trainer Battle starts

**Issue Description:**  After finding an opponent and right before the battle starts, some Trainers may find that their screen has frozen on a white screen and are unable to participate in the battle.

**Issue Status:** Fixed in future release

When a Fast Attack and Charged Attack happen simultaneously, damage from the Fast Attack may be inflicted first

**Issue Description:**  If the opponent’s Fast Attack is activated when a Charged Attack button is tapped, Fast Attack damage from the opponent Pokémon may be inflicted before the Charged Attack minigame starts.

**Issue Status:**  Fixed in future release

Fast Attacks do not activate when a Trainer taps the screen with multiple inputs

**Issue Description:**  When a Trainer attempts to activate Fast Attacks by tapping their screen with more than one input, such as multiple fingers, the Fast Attack may not be performed consistently.

**Issue Status:**  Fixed in future release

After a Pokémon is switched in, its first Fast Attack’s animation may not be shown

**Issue Description:** The first Fast Attack performed by a Pokémon immediately after being switched in may not have the usual Fast Attack animation. This is a visual issue only and all effects of the Fast Attack are still applied.

**Issue Status:**  Fixed in future release

CP changing Pokémon do not get the expected resulting CP after transforming

**Issue Description:**  CP changing Pokémon, such as Aegislash, do not get the expected resulting CP after their transformation.

**Issue Status:**  Fixed in future release

When Mimikyu’s Disguise breaks, changes in its Stats do not visually match effects from Stat Changing Charged Attacks

**Issue Description:**  After Mimikyu’s Disguise breaks, its Defense Stat does not visually match Stat changes from Defense-lowering Charged Attacks. This is a visual issue only and Mimikyu’s Defense Stat is still affected by Defense-lowering Charged Attacks.

**Issue Status:**  Fixed in future release

## Resolved

After a Trainer’s second Pokémon faints, the last Pokémon switched in and its Charged Attack buttons may not visually appear

**Issue Description:**  When a Trainer’s second Pokémon faints and their last Pokémon is switched in, the last Pokémon may not visually appear on the battlefield and the Charged Attack buttons may still show the previous fainted Pokémon’s Charged Attacks.

**Issue Status:**  Resolved

After a Trainer’s second Pokémon faints, the Charged Attack of the Pokémon switched in may activate immediately

**Issue Description:**  When a Trainer’s last Pokémon is switched in after their previous Pokémon faints, the Charged Attack of the Pokémon switched in may activate immediately without the Trainer tapping on the Charged Attack button.

**Issue Status:**  Resolved

Battle may pause for a few seconds after completing a Charged Attack minigame

**Issue Description:**  Trainers may find that, after completing a Charged Attack minigame, the battle may pause for a few seconds before the Charged Attack activates and that they are unable to perform any actions during this pause.

**Issue Status:**  Resolved

Unintended Fast Attack may activate when switching a Pokémon in

**Issue Description:**  If a Trainer taps on the switch menu on the side rapidly, an unintended Fast Attack may activate after the Pokémon is switched in.

**Issue Status:** Resolved

Rematches for battles made from the QR matchmaking function may result in an immediate win or loss

**Issue Description:** When two Trainers use the QR matchmaking function to battle each other, tapping Rematch after the first battle may result in an immediate win or loss for the Trainers without the battle actually taking place.

**Issue Status:**  Resolved

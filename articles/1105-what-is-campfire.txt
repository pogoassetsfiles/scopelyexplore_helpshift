**Campfire**  is a new social app that helps Scopely Explore Explorers discover new people, places and experiences around them! Discover in-game activities, plan your next meetup, send a DM, and manage your friends list, all within a single app. Some features of Campfire are available within Scopely Explore’s games, but for the full experience, users should download the standalone app.

**Issue description:**  Some Trainers who purchased GO Pass Deluxe: Twilight Wishes +6 Ranks through the Pokémon GO Web Store before the GO Pass began in their local time did not receive the additional six ranks included with their purchase.
 **Status:**  Resolved. Affected Trainers have been granted the missing six ranks.

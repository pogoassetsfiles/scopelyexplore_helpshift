**Issue Description:**  Trainers who have completed the "Explore X km" evolution tasks required to evolve certain Pokémon (e.g. Bramblin, Pawmo) are unable to evolve it.
 **Issue Status:**  Working on a fix. Any Pokémon that have met the eligibility will be able to evolve once the fix has been deployed.

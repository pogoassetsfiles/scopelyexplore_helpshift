**Issue description:** Trainers who leave a remote Mega Raid or are defeated in one may be unable to rejoin it, even though their Remote Raid Pass was used.

**Issue status:** A fix is planned for version 433.1. In the meantime, affected Trainers can contact Support in the app to request a replacement Remote Raid Pass.
